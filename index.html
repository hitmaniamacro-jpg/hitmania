How it works

What it actually does

Six things that matter when you're leaving it running for hours.

Finds the bar, not a pixel

Template matching with OpenCV finds the click-fast bar on screen and clicks its centre. If the UI shifts, it still lands where it should.

No dead time

Reeling stops the moment the bar disappears and the reward popup is confirmed, so the next cast starts almost immediately.

Never clicks early

Clicks wait until the bar has matched on several consecutive frames. A click before the bar appears would reset your cast.

Window can move

Locks Roblox to a fixed size, then re-aligns every calibrated point by itself whenever the window moves or changes size.

Posts to Discord

Every catch can go to a webhook with a screenshot attached, plus hourly and on-stop session summaries. Sends never slow the fishing loop.

Hotkeys that work

F6 start/stop, F7 capture bar, F8 capture reward, F9 set cast point. All of them work while Roblox has focus, and all of them are rebindable.

Setup

Five minutes, three keys

Hitmania saves its settings next to the exe, so once you've calibrated it you never touch the setup again.

  1. 1

    Unzip it somewhere permanent

    Documents\Hitmania is fine. It saves its settings next to the exe, so moving the folder later means calibrating again.

  2. 2

    Run it, ignore the warning

    Windows will say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't code-signed. More info, then Run anyway.

  3. 3

    Calibrate — three keys

    Open Setup. Aim at the water and press F9, hover the bar and press F7, hover a reward popup and press F8.

  4. 4

    Press F6

    Then click the Roblox window so it has focus. That's it — it fishes from there.

Worth knowing

What it won’t do

Most macro sites hide this part. You’d find out anyway eventually, so here it is up front.

It won't survive a UI change

The bar and reward are matched against templates — screenshots of what Roblox currently looks like. When the game updates its interface, you re-capture them with F7 and F8. Ten seconds of work, but you have to do it.

It can't run Roblox for you

You still have to open the game and be in a server. Hitmania clicks and casts; it doesn't log in, join a game, or buy bait.

No anti-detection tricks

It sends normal mouse and key events through the Windows API. There's no driver, no kernel stuff, no attempt to hide itself. That's why some antivirus tools complain, and it's also why I can't promise you won't get flagged by the game.

One PC, one window

It only drives the Roblox window you've calibrated. Running two instances means calibrating and running them one at a time.
