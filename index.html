How it works

What it actually does

Six things that matter when you're leaving it running for hours.

Finds the bar, not a pixel

Template matching with OpenCV finds the click-fast bar on screen and clicks its centre. The bar and the catch popup come with the macro, so there is nothing to capture before your first run.

Steps out of the way

Press Start and the big window folds into a small badge in the corner with your catch count, the clock and a Stop button. The game stays visible; the badge never covers it.

Never wastes a cast

Clicks wait until the bar has matched on several consecutive frames, and after a catch it waits two full seconds before casting again. Clicking early, or casting into a popup that's still animating away, resets the cast.

One point to set up

Aim at the water, press F9. That's the whole setup — no framing, no region boxes, no four-step calibration. It saves the point next to the exe and reuses it every run.

Posts to Discord

Every catch can go to a webhook with a screenshot attached, plus hourly and on-stop session summaries. Sends never slow the fishing loop.

Two keys, both rebindable

F6 starts and stops, F9 marks the water. Both work while Roblox has focus. The capture and rod keys are still there for game updates, just unbound until you need them.

Setup

One minute, two keys

Hitmania saves its settings next to the exe, so the cast point is the only thing you ever set. Everything else is already done.

  1. 1

    Unzip it somewhere permanent

    Documents\Hitmania is fine. It saves its settings next to the exe, so moving the folder later means setting the cast point again.

  2. 2

    Run it, ignore the warning

    Windows will say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't code-signed. More info, then Run anyway.

  3. 3

    Mark where you fish

    Open Steal an Egg, aim at the water, then press F9 (or click Set cast point in Setup). One point and it's done — the bar and the catch popup are already recognised.

  4. 4

    Press Start fishing

    Equip your rod first, then press Start fishing or F6. The window folds into the corner badge and it fishes from there. F6 stops it.

Worth knowing

What it won’t do

Most macro sites hide this part. You’d find out anyway eventually, so here it is up front.

It won't survive a big UI change

The bar and the catch popup are matched against templates that ship inside the exe. When Roblox redraws its interface they stop matching, and you capture new ones from Setup → Advanced → Detection, or wait for me to ship an update. Ten seconds of work, but you have to do it.

It can't run Roblox for you

You still have to open the game and be in a server. Hitmania clicks and casts; it doesn't log in, join a game, or buy bait.

No anti-detection tricks

It sends normal mouse and key events through the Windows API. There's no driver, no kernel stuff, no attempt to hide itself. That's why some antivirus tools complain, and it's also why I can't promise you won't get flagged by the game.

One PC, one window

It only drives the Roblox window you pointed it at. Running two instances means setting them up and running them one at a time.
